# JobTrail privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

JobTrail is a browser extension that keeps a log of the jobs you apply to on LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri. This page explains what it stores and where.

## What JobTrail stores

When you click Apply on a job page on linkedin.com, indeed.com or naukri.com, JobTrail reads that page and saves:

- the job title, company, location, salary and employment details shown on the page
- the job description
- the link to the posting and which site it came from
- the date you applied, and the date each time you change the job's status

You can also add your own notes to a job: interview notes, the resume version you sent and your expected salary.

JobTrail only reads job pages on those three sites, and only to save the job you applied to. It does not read other websites, your browsing history, or anything on those sites beyond the job details.

## Where it is stored

Everything is saved in your browser's local extension storage, on your own computer. It is not synced to your Google account, and there is no JobTrail server or account.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. JobTrail makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party services, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Two features move data only when you ask them to:

- **Export** saves your jobs to a JSON file on your computer.
- **Copy as prompt** copies one job's details and your notes to your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you.

## Notifications

Twice a day JobTrail checks your saved jobs and, if any need a follow-up, shows a desktop notification. This check runs on your computer and sends nothing anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Delete a single job from its detail view. To delete everything, remove the extension from Chrome; its storage is removed with it.
